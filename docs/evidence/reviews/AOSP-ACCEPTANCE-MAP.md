Limits that remain even if the minimum harness passes:

- Same-UID isolation uses two live Binder tokens but targets a **completed** foreign golden job. It does not assert foreign cancellation of a RUNNING job. The distinct-UID denial fixture is separate.
- Live cancellation accepts CANCELLED or prior SUCCEEDED and checks terminal immutability. The barrier-controlled native-engine tests separately establish cancellation/deadline winning; successful completion is not relabeled as a cancellation win.
- FD/core measurements demonstrate quiescent recovery in one same-generation cycle. They do not establish peak-resource bounds, continuous leak freedom, balanced-descriptor replacement detection or soak behaviour.
- The denied client reaches the UID gate through deliberate SELinux discovery/call permissions. Typed DENIED is not a SELinux discovery-denial result.
- Six selected generated vectors and bounded edge cases are not exhaustive equivalence with every JVM/shared-corpus case. The minimum facade lifecycle check uses a fresh process after restart; a surviving client’s transport-loss observation is not independently exercised.
- P06 previously compiled-consumer evolution, P07 controlled performance, P08 two-hour soak and P09 fresh final-image reproduction are intentionally excluded. This review does not propose another runtime campaign.
